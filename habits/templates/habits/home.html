THE CONSISTIFY SCORE

A meaningful number.
With the math to match.

Your Consistify Score is a 0–100 measure built from your scheduled habit sessions. It’s calculated from meaningful habit data, never randomly assigned. Here’s what goes into it.

Completion quality 35%

How much of your scheduled work you complete on average. Partial progress counts, too.

Full completion 20%

The share of scheduled sessions you finish at 100%. Credit for following through.

Consistency rhythm 30%

Your participation and continuity across your last seven scheduled sessions.

Recent momentum 15%

The direction of your last six scheduled sessions: improving, declining, or holding steady.

How history and habit priority affect your score +

Rhythm and momentum need a history to draw on. Their influence grows with the number of scheduled sessions in your selected period, reaching full influence at seven sessions. Completion quality and full completion keep their stated weights from the start.

Habit score = 0.35 × quality + 0.20 × full completion + evidence × (0.30 × rhythm + 0.15 × momentum)

Each signal is measured from 0 to 100. The evidence factor grows from 0 to 1 as your scheduled history builds. With no scheduled sessions, the score is 0. Momentum reflects recent direction, with reduced credit when your latest session has very little progress.

Your overall score is a weighted average of your habit scores. Each habit’s weight is its average priority multiplied by the square root of its scheduled-session count. That gives your priorities and the amount of habit history a defined role in the result.

In the app, this is labeled your Consistency Score. Its breakdown shows how each signal contributes, so you can understand the number and what changes it.
